Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log a meal?

Type what you ate into the chat bar — for example, "2 eggs and toast with butter." Nomva's AI will parse the foods, match them to our database, and log the nutrition automatically.

Can I scan barcodes?

Yes. Tap the barcode icon next to the chat input to open the scanner. Nomva checks its built-in local food database directly on your device.

How do I change my calorie and macro goals?

Go to Settings > Goals. You can set custom targets or let Nomva suggest values based on your profile and activity level.

Does Nomva work with Garmin?

Yes. Connect your Garmin account in Settings to have active calories adjust your daily calorie target and, if you choose, import Garmin body-weight history into Nomva. Garmin's public API does not allow third-party apps to write weights back to Garmin.

Is my data private?

Your food logs stay on your device (and your private iCloud if sync is enabled). Apple Health activity and weight access stay on-device as well. Garmin activity and optional weight-history imports pass through Nomva Cloud so the app can receive them; imported Garmin weights are not retained by Nomva Cloud. AI chat and meal parsing also pass through our server and then to our AI provider so those features can work. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I restore my purchase?

Go to Settings > Membership and tap "Restore Purchases." This will re-activate any previous Nomva Pro subscription tied to your Apple ID.

How do I export my data?

Go to Settings > Backup & Export. You can export a CSV report for your coach or a full JSON backup of all your data.
