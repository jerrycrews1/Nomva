Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log a meal?

Open Log and tap Add Food to search the included catalog, review a serving, and save it to a meal. AI Chat can help with meal descriptions when you have an active Nomva Pro subscription and cloud access is available.

Can I scan barcodes?

Yes. On Android, open Add Food and tap the scanner icon, or type a barcode into the same food search field. On iPhone, use the barcode control in the food logging flow. Nomva checks its local catalog and cache, then may query Open Food Facts for a missing product.

How do I change my calorie and macro goals?

On Android, open Settings > Calorie & Macro Goals; on iPhone, open Settings > Goals. Adults can review a general estimate based on profile and activity. Nomva does not provide an individualized medical diet plan, and its automatic targets do not go below 1,500 kcal/day. This product floor is not a guarantee that a target is appropriate for you. If you are under 18, pregnant, breastfeeding, recovering from an eating disorder, or have a medical condition, ask a qualified clinician before choosing a target.

Can Nomva give medical or allergy advice?

No. Food records, photos, AI responses, and wearable activity are estimates. Nomva cannot diagnose or treat a condition, adjust medication, or confirm a food is allergy-safe. Check current packaging and ask a qualified clinician for medical decisions. For a severe allergic reaction or other emergency, follow your emergency plan and seek urgent local medical help.

Where does the food database come from?

The bundled catalog combines USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts. Read the source and license notice and download the combined database there.

Does Nomva work with Garmin?

An optional Garmin connection can supply daily activity summaries through Nomva Cloud. On iPhone, Garmin weight history follows Garmin Connect → Apple Health → Nomva; Nomva weigh-ins can go back to Apple Health, but are not written to Garmin. On Android, weight and active-calorie sharing uses Health Connect with your permission. Direct Garmin weight import is not provided.

Is my data private?

Food, water, and weight logs stay in the app's local storage unless you export or explicitly send information through a cloud feature. Automatic device backup is disabled for app history. On iPhone, Apple Health measurements are read on-device; on Android, Health Connect permissions control weight and activity access. Optional Garmin activity and AI features use Nomva Cloud. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I restore my purchase?

Go to Settings > Membership and tap Restore Purchases. On iPhone this checks the Apple Account used for Nomva Pro; on Android it checks the Google Play account used for Nomva Pro. An expired subscription is not renewed by restoring. If access does not return, check your subscription status with the store used for the purchase.

How do I export my data?

Go to Settings > Backup & Export. You can export a CSV report for your coach or a full JSON backup of all your data.

Why does my calorie target differ from calories burned today?

Your starting goal already includes an activity baseline from completed days. Today's partial activity does not lower that baseline. Only activity above the baseline earns extra calories, so the same activity is not counted twice. Corrected activity totals can reduce a previously shown bonus. Missing days are not treated as zero. Select one source in Settings > Goals and pull down in the Log tab to refresh it.

Activity or weight is missing or delayed

First verify the measurement in its source app. On iPhone, confirm Garmin Connect has synced and Apple Health sharing permits Nomva to read Active Energy and/or Weight; use Weight Sync > Sync Now or Recheck All Health History when appropriate. On Android, grant the matching Health Connect permissions, enable import or activity reading in Weight History Sync, and tap Sync Now. The Android app reads the most recent 30 days and does not request older history. A missing result can mean no readable records or a permissions issue.

Chat is slow or a request failed

Nomva retries a transient connection failure once for food-processing requests. A request that exceeds its time limit stops with recovery options. Retry or use Search locally after a failure. If Nomva closed while saving a request, review your log before sending it again. When reporting a problem, include the app version, approximate time, what you expected, and whether you changed networks or backgrounded the app. Do not send private health records or account credentials.

Will my history survive reinstalling or changing phones?

Export a JSON backup first using Settings > Backup & Export, and keep the file outside Nomva. Local app history is excluded from automatic device backup; CSV is for reading, not restoring. Android and iPhone JSON backup formats are different and cannot be restored across platforms. Both restores merge records; the iPhone app makes a local recovery copy before restore, while the Android app does not. Apple Health and Health Connect histories are separate from your Nomva food and chat history. Exported files contain personal information, so choose a destination you trust.
