Your Data, Your Control

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 18, 2026

Overview

Nomva is a nutrition tracking app designed to help you log meals, monitor macros, and reach your health goals. Your privacy matters, and this policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Data We Collect

Nomva stores your food log entries, daily goals, weight entries, and custom foods in protected local storage on your device. When you use AI-powered features such as meal parsing or chat, the text you type, along with limited recent AI context needed to answer correctly, is sent through Nomva's server to our AI provider for processing.

Health & Fitness Data

If you connect Apple Health, Nomva reads activity data (such as active calories burned) and, when you enable weight history import, body-weight samples on your device. If you enable weight export, Nomva writes weights you log in the app to Apple Health. Automatically imported weights, their notes, and personal goal summaries are excluded from AI requests. Weight history questions and measurements are handled locally. Apple Health data is not sent to Nomva's servers. Garmin data is handled separately through Nomva Cloud as described below.

Garmin Connect

If you choose to connect your Garmin account, Nomva receives daily activity summaries (active calories) through the Garmin Health API. Current versions import Garmin weigh-ins through Apple Health on the device. The earlier direct Garmin weight-import endpoint is retired. Legacy body-composition deliveries are discarded; Nomva does not maintain a server weight cache. We store the Garmin credentials needed to maintain your connection in encrypted form on our server. No Garmin data is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Third-Party Services

Nomva uses OpenAI's API to power AI features. AI chat and meal-parsing text is relayed through Nomva's server and then sent to OpenAI for processing. We do not intentionally store the content of those AI conversations on our server after processing, and we avoid logging raw prompt and response text in production. Text sent for processing is also subject to OpenAI's data usage policies. Barcode lookups check the bundled food database and a device cache first. For missing products, the barcode is sent directly to Open Food Facts to request product nutrition. Open Food Facts data is attributed under its ODbL license. No chat or weight history is sent with that request.

Weight Sync and Local Storage

Nomva keeps its application history on this device. Weight sync uses Apple Health, including Garmin measurements shared to Apple Health by Garmin Connect. To exchange weights across Apple devices, enable Health in iCloud on those devices and grant Nomva Weight access. Nomva does not store a server-side weight-sync database. Existing on-device records are preserved. Copies synced by earlier Nomva versions to your private iCloud account are not deleted automatically; you can manage them in Apple Settings.

Analytics & Advertising

Nomva does not include third-party analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, or advertising frameworks, and we do not sell your data. Our service keeps limited operational metadata, such as hashed app identifiers, route, timing, status, model, and token counts, for up to 90 days. These diagnostics do not store raw chat text, photos, food names, barcodes, or weight values. You can remove linked diagnostics in Settings → AI & Privacy → Delete Cloud Analytics.

Data Retention

Your food logs and weight history are stored locally; Nomva does not maintain a server-side copy for history sync. The service retains the authentication, subscription-verification, and request-limit state needed to protect AI access. If you enable the separate Garmin activity connection, its encrypted credentials and daily activity summaries are stored as described above. AI requests pass through our server for processing, but we do not intentionally retain full AI message content after the response is returned.

Your Rights

Removing the app deletes its local history. Use AI & Privacy settings to delete linked cloud diagnostics. Disconnecting Garmin in Nomva removes the server connection and its cached activity summaries. Copies created by older iCloud-sync versions can be managed in Apple Settings. Apple Health data has separate controls in the Health app. To disconnect Garmin, visit the Garmin Connect app or website.
