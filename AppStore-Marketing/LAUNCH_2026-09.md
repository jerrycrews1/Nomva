# Nomva 1.6 promotion plan

Prepared September 22, 2026. This is the working launch plan for the public iPhone app. The older `Nomva_CPP_Plan.docx` and images in `output/` are archival; they were made from earlier builds and must not be used for this launch.

## Launch decision

**Start with the food logging problem:** people who want to track meals but find searching and entering every ingredient tedious. Show one honest meal entry, the estimate, the ability to correct it, and the daily calorie and macro result. The secondary audience is people who already use Apple Health or Garmin activity; lead with that only after the device checks below.

**Offer:** Nomva is free to download. Manual food search and barcode logging are available in the app. AI meal chat and photo logging require Nomva Pro; the US App Store lists Pro Monthly at $4.99 as of September 22. Always let the App Store and the in-app StoreKit paywall display the buyer's local price. There is no free AI message allowance in the current production code and no introductory trial configured in this plan.

**Main CTA:** [Download Nomva on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nomva/id6762495287). The website's two Download buttons now use an exact Apple-generated campaign URL; channel-specific links are recorded in `CAMPAIGN_LINKS_2026-09.md`. Do not invent a provider token or imply that manually tagged URLs will appear in Apple's Campaigns report.

**First-month spend:** $0 organic baseline. If a budget is approved, add a small paid test only after the purchase, onboarding, and retention checks are healthy. No ad account, campaign, code giveaway, creator payment, or message send is part of this prepared plan.

**First revenue milestone:** 10 genuine paying Pro subscribers, then measure how many renew and why others do not. At the current US list price this is $49.90 in monthly customer charges before taxes, Apple's commission, refunds, and territory differences; use App Store Connect **proceeds** for the actual amount payable. This is a validation milestone, not a sales forecast. [Apple's proceeds definition](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/getting-paid/view-payments-and-proceeds).

**What is already true:** the US App Store listing currently shows version 1.6, free with in-app purchases, and the $4.99/month US Pro product. The public listing has one rating, so there is little social proof yet. The website now has a live Download CTA and clear Pro note. The 1.6 release evidence records automated checks and an installed device build, while several physical Health/Garmin and StoreKit paths remain unverified. Live App Store status supersedes the older release document's “Waiting for Review” text. See `LAUNCH_STATUS_2026-09.md` for current execution status.

## 2. Build the asset set

Make one reusable 15–20 second vertical recording from the actual 1.6 build:

1. Start on an empty or clean day. Type an ordinary meal in natural language (for example, “turkey sandwich and an apple”).
2. Show the AI estimate appearing. Pause on the food and portion review; demonstrate one correction rather than presenting the first estimate as exact.
3. End on the updated calorie and macro totals. Add a small final card: “Free download · AI logging with Nomva Pro.”

Create a second short recording of manual search or barcode lookup, with a real supported item and the resulting log entry. This establishes what users can try without paying. A photo demo can replace it after confirming the camera permission and real capture flow on the public build.

Refresh the App Store and social screenshot set from the public 1.6 build. Suggested sequence: (1) type a meal and review estimates, (2) correct portions, (3) daily calories/macros, (4) barcode/manual logging, (5) weight or hydration; use activity only after its physical check. Keep overlays short and true. The April screenshot generator and its `output/` images are retired because the UI and privacy/sync claims have changed. Apple requires app previews to use footage captured from the app; keep any App Store preview within Apple's current format and length rules. [Apple app preview guidance](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-previews/) and [App Review guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/).

Keep a small press folder with the app icon, approved screenshots, the two recordings, the one-sentence description, the App Store link, support contact, and a note that AI nutrition is estimated. No review score graphics from the present single rating. Request permission before reusing user quotes or before/after health stories.

## 3. Prepare the destinations and tracking

1. **Website:** the `server/public/index.html` update is live and link-checked. Keep its App Store CTA, support and privacy links, and sentence that AI needs Pro. The current privacy policy says app history is local, manual export is available, and AI submissions use cloud processing; do not say all data stays on device, automatic iCloud history sync exists, or backups happen automatically.
2. **App Store:** use the existing listing for the first push. Review the first three screenshots on a small phone: they should show the core meal flow and readable copy before feature breadth. Preserve the current accurate description of the selected activity source, local history, manual backup, and AI estimates. Do not change metadata solely for keyword density.
3. **Campaign links:** in App Store Connect, open Apps → Nomva → Analytics → Acquisition → Campaigns and create a separate link for each major channel once Campaigns is available. Suggested campaign names: `launch_site_sep26`, `launch_ig_demo_sep26`, `launch_personal_sep26`, `launch_creator_sep26`. Paste the exact generated links into the final posts and the tracking log. Apple says the feature may not appear until the app has analytics data; individual campaign data needs at least five first-time downloads to appear. Until links exist, use the plain App Store URL and mark source attribution as uncertain. [Apple campaign links](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect-analytics/acquisition/campaign-links).
4. **Baseline:** before posting, capture the last available 7 and 30 days of App Store impressions, product page views, first-time downloads, download conversion, subscription starts, proceeds, and retention from App Store Connect. Add daily support contacts and qualitative feedback without collecting health details. These are the comparison point for the first month. [Apple analytics overview](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect-analytics/overview/analytics-dashboard).
5. **No analytics overclaim:** current server telemetry covers reliability, not a complete marketing funnel. Do not report onboarding completion, first meal rate, paywall conversion, or channel-level subscriber value unless the necessary measurements are actually implemented and privacy-reviewed.

## 4. Four-week organic rollout

| When | Action | What to observe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prep, days 1–3 | Complete purchase/recovery checks; publish website CTA; capture current screenshots and clips; record App Store baseline. | Broken purchase/link paths or misleading copy stop distribution. |
| Week 1 | Publish the first Nomva brand post and natural-language meal demo from a dedicated Nomva account; invite relevant, willing testers to give *honest usability feedback*. Reply to support quickly. | Link clicks if available, first-time downloads, repeated questions, crashes, refund/support reports. |
| Week 2 | Publish the free manual/barcode demo. Share one useful “how I review an AI food estimate” educational post. Join a small number of relevant discussions with genuine advice and disclose your connection if mentioning Nomva. Check each community's promotion rules. | Which promise attracts interested users and which screen creates confusion. |
| Week 3 | Send up to five personalized, relevant creator/reviewer pitches after reviewing their recent content. Offer a factual demo and a free access code only if an offer is configured and tested. Disclose any payment or free product. Publish an approved user quote only with explicit permission. | Replies, qualified conversations, source-linked downloads. |
| Week 4 | Publish one follow-up based on actual feedback or a product fix. Compare this month with baseline and review subscription starts and proceeds. Choose one next experiment: improve the first screenshot, improve the paywall/onboarding, or test the Apple Health/Garmin angle after device acceptance. | A decision based on observed bottleneck, with sample size noted. |

Suggested cadence: two helpful posts per week, one product demo per week, daily responses to comments/support during launch week. Recut the same truthful capture for each platform's format; do not manufacture progress photos or user testimonials.

## 5. Decide what to change from the results

Record raw counts and date ranges before interpreting percentages. A single rating or a few installs cannot establish conversion performance. Compare each source only when Apple displays enough data, and never infer a source for unattributed purchases.

| Observation | First action |
| --- | --- |
| Few product page views | Improve distribution: clearer hook, relevant creator conversations, and more demonstrations. Do not change price based on near-zero traffic. |
| Views but few downloads | Inspect the first screenshot, app subtitle, price disclosure, and match between post and listing. Consider Apple's product page optimization only after there is enough traffic for useful results. |
| Downloads but little Pro revenue | Observe a real first-use session and test purchase/restore path; ask what users expected from free versus Pro. Fix confusion or reliability before changing price. |
| Good trials or purchases but weak continuation | Investigate estimate corrections, daily usefulness, and support complaints. Improve the product before buying more traffic. |

Apple offers [product page optimization](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect-analytics/acquisition/product-page-optimization) and [custom product pages](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/create-custom-product-pages/configure-multiple-product-page-versions). Use them later for a tested angle and current screenshots, with review time in the schedule. Do not reuse the old plan's claim of a 35-page limit or a fixed impression count that supposedly proves a winner. A paid Apple Ads experiment is optional only after a budget, campaign cap, and success criterion are set; Apple notes daily budgets can spend unevenly across days. [Apple Ads budgets](https://ads.apple.com/app-store/help/bids-and-budget/0016-manage-budgets).

An introductory trial or offer code is a **second experiment**, not a launch promise. Apple supports both [introductory offers](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-subscriptions/set-up-introductory-offers-for-auto-renewable-subscriptions) and [subscription offer codes](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-subscriptions/set-up-subscription-offer-codes). Before creating one, decide who qualifies, duration, renewal price, and a maximum number of codes; update and test the paywall so its price and offer terms are clear, then verify redemption and expiry on device. The current paywall simply shows the StoreKit monthly price, so an untested trial could make the screen misleading.
