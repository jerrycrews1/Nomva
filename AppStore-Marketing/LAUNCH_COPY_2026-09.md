## Answers to common questions

**Is it free?** “Nomva is free to download, and manual food logging is available without Pro. AI meal chat and photo logging require Nomva Pro. The US App Store currently lists Pro Monthly at $4.99; check your App Store for local pricing.”

**How accurate is the AI?** “Nutrition results are estimates. Review matched foods and portions and correct anything that looks wrong.”

**Where is my history?** “Your app history is stored locally. Export a JSON backup in Settings → Backup & Export before replacing your phone or reinstalling. AI messages and chosen photos are sent for cloud processing when you use those features. The [privacy policy](https://nomva.nerdquad.com/privacy.html) has details.”

**Does Garmin weight sync directly?** “Garmin weight can reach Nomva through Apple Health when the sharing permissions are set up. The optional direct Garmin connection is for activity summaries.”

**Can I cancel?** “Nomva Pro is a monthly App Store subscription. Manage or cancel it in your Apple subscription settings.”
