# Support for Nomva

Thank you for using Nomva.

Nomva is designed to make nutrition tracking simple by letting you log food with AI chat or manual entry. If you need help, support is available.

## Contact

For support questions, bug reports, feature requests, or account-related issues, please contact:

**Email:** jerry@nerdquad.com

## What to Include

To help resolve your issue faster, please include:

- Your device model
- Your iOS version
- Your Nomva app version
- A short description of the issue
- Screenshots, if relevant

## Common Support Topics

Support is available for issues including:

- AI food logging not working correctly
- Incorrect nutrition calculations
- Problems saving meals or weight entries
- iCloud sync issues
- App crashes or unexpected behavior
- Questions about settings, goals, or data deletion

## Response Time

We aim to respond to support requests as quickly as possible, typically within 2 to 5 business days.

## Privacy

If you contact support, any information you send will only be used to help troubleshoot and resolve your issue.

## Feature Requests

Suggestions for improving Nomva are welcome. If there is a feature you would like to see, please send it through the support email above.
